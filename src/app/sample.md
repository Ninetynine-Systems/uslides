# Welcome to uslides

Write slides as plain Markdown. They appear on the right as you type.

---

# The basics

A line with `---` on its own starts a new slide.

- Bullets, **bold** and *italic* work as usual
- A `#` heading is the slide title
- Everything is saved in this browser

---

::: box col=1-6 row=2-6 bg=#e0ecff
## Left box

Boxes sit on a grid of 12 columns and 6 rows.
:::

::: box col=7-12 row=2-6 bg=#fef3c7 valign=middle
## Right box

Set `col`, `row`, `bg` and `align` to place and style each one.
:::
