# Welcome to uslides ✨

## Write it. See it. Show it.

Slides from plain text, right in your browser. No account, nothing to install, and it works offline.

👉 Double-click any text on a slide to change it, or type on the left.

---

<!-- slide bg=#fff4e6 color=#4a2511 -->

# The basics

A line with `---` on its own starts a new slide.

- **Bold**, *italic* and `code` work as usual
- A `#` heading is the slide title
- Lists, quotes, tables and links too 🎈

> Everything is saved in this browser, and nowhere else.

::: box col=8-12 row=2-6 bg=#ffd8a8 color=#4a2511
### What you type

```
# My talk

- One idea per line
- **Bold** the key word
```
:::
